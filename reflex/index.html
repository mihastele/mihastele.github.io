The Science of Reflex

1. Psychomotor Vigilance (PVT): Measures visual pathway conduction from retinal photoreceptors via the optic tract to primary motor cortex actuation. The human physiological limit is roughly 140–180ms.

2. Go / No-Go Inhibition: Engages the prefrontal cortex to assess executive impulse suppression. Inhibiting response to decoy cues exercises neuro-motor brake circuits.

3. Hick-Hyman Law: Reaction time increases logarithmically as the number of spatial alternatives increases ($RT = a + b \log_2(n)$). Spatial Grid trains saccadic eye movement and peripheral attention.
